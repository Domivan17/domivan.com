Questions

How is the price set?

The app works out the price from your pickup and destination, the van class and the number of seats. You see the full price before you pay, and it doesn't change afterwards.

When do I see who's driving?

Ahead of your pickup the app shows your driver, the van and its number plate, and you can chat or call them. On the day you can follow the van on the map once it's heading your way.

What if no transporter takes my trip?

You get your money back in full, automatically.

Can I cancel?

Yes. How much you get back depends on how close to pickup you cancel. See the terms for travellers.

How do I get help?

Open the app and go to Help and support. Our team answers there.
