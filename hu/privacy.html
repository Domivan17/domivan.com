We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use automated decision-making that has legal effects on you. Offers are shown to all eligible transporters at the same time and go to whoever accepts first.

Chat translation. When the traveller and the transporter use the app in different languages, each new chat message is translated automatically into the reader's app language by Google Cloud Translation, a machine translation service (not an AI chatbot). The reader sees the translation with a "Show original" link; the original message is kept unchanged and is what our support staff see. Each person can turn translation off in Profile, under "Translate chat messages"; their incoming messages then stay in the original. A message is translated only for a reader who has it on, and only when the two app languages differ. We stop translating for the rest of the month if a monthly cost limit is reached.

Location. The traveller app does not track your location. Addresses and towns you type are turned into map coordinates by Photon and Nominatim, two search services based on OpenStreetMap, or, when they find nothing, by your phone's built-in map service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). They receive what you typed for that purpose. The Driver app uses your location in two cases. While a trip is in the "Towards client" stage, it shares your position with that traveller and keeps only your latest position for that trip, visible only to you and that traveller. While you are Online, it sends your approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) so trip requests that start near you reach you; we keep only the latest one and delete it when you go offline or Online ends.

3. Who we share it with

  • The other side of your trip. Your transporter or traveller sees your name, phone number and photo from about 24 hours before pickup (right away for Live trips), plus the trip details and your chat. Transporters see the trip route, date and passengers, without names, when it is offered.
  • The DomiVan partner who invited you, if you signed up with their invite code. They see your name (or company name), when you joined, how many trips you completed and what they earned from them, never your contact details, addresses, trip prices or messages.
  • Service providers who process data for us:
    • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and servers), hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
    • Stripe (payments and transporter payouts). For transporter payouts, Stripe also collects identity and bank details itself, as a separate controller under its own privacy policy.
    • Lettermint (sending sign-in code emails).
    • Expo (delivering push notifications), with Apple and Google, which deliver notifications to your phone.
    • Google Cloud (Cloud Translation), for translating chat messages. We use its EU endpoint, so messages are processed only in the European Union. Google keeps a message only in memory while translating it, does not use it to train its models and does not share it, under the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum.
    • Apple and Google (app stores, and turning addresses into map coordinates on your phone).
    • komoot (Photon) and the OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim), for finding the addresses and towns you type. When a transporter taps "Use my location" in Trip preferences, Photon receives only a position rounded to about 1 km, to name the town.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of passengers.

4. Transfers outside the EU

Our main database is in the EU, and chat messages are translated in the EU. Some providers (such as Stripe, Expo, Apple and Google) may process data in the United States or other countries. 5. How long we keep it

  • Account details: as long as your account exists. Deleting your account in the app removes them right away.
  • Trips you booked but never paid for: their pickup and drop-off addresses, map coordinates and notes are deleted 30 days after you made them, or right away when you delete your account. The towns, date and price stay.
  • Join Transporters applications: the name, phone number, email, company name, registration and VAT numbers and our staff's notes are wiped 24 months after the application reached us when no DomiVan account uses its email address, or earlier if you ask us. When you delete your DomiVan account, those on applications sent with the same email address are wiped right away. The record of each application stays: when it came, what we decided, and the answers that do not name you.
  • Our internal staff log: kept as the record of what our staff decided and when. When you delete your account, everything personal in it about you, your applications and your trips is removed, also the reasons our staff typed; it then keeps only what was decided and when, without personal details. The texts of error reports in it are deleted after 90 days.
  • Invites: which partner invited you, kept with the payout records of what it earned them. The phone ID sent with the code: [DECIDE: delete it 12 months after sign-up, or when the account is deleted.]
  • Trip, payment and payout records: 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires.
  • Chat messages and their translations: 1 year after the trip is completed or cancelled, unless a complaint about the trip is still open.
  • Chat translations also go as soon as either person on the trip deletes their account, and those from while a transporter had the trip as soon as that transporter, who then gave it back, deletes theirs (the original messages stay with the trip).
  • Support messages: 1 year after they were sent, unless your latest message is still waiting for our answer or a complaint about the trip it is about is still open. Questions the help assistant could not answer: 1 year after we have dealt with them.
  • Live location: only the latest position, kept with the trip record. [DECIDE: delete live location when the trip is completed.]
  • Online position: only the latest one, deleted within an hour after you go offline or Online ends.
  • Transporter documents: the photos are deleted 1 year after you last used DomiVan Driver (signed in or opened the app, had a trip or uploaded a document), or right away when you delete your account. The record of which documents we approved and when stays.
  • Sign-in security logs (your email and IP address with each sign-in): deleted after 90 days, and your entries right away when you delete your account.
  • Server logs: at most 90 days. Error reports from our servers and apps: deleted 90 days after the error was last seen.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict how we use it, or object to our use based on legitimate interest. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can change your name, phone and photo yourself in the app. To use your other rights, write to us through Help and support or at privacy@domivan.com; we answer within one month.

You can delete your account yourself in the app under Profile, Delete account. See Delete your account.

You can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the authority in your own country.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to the database is limited by strict per-user rules, sign-in uses one-time codes instead of passwords, and card details are handled only by Stripe. No system is perfectly safe; if a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

You must be 18 or older to have an account. Travellers may book for children travelling with them; we receive only the number of passengers, not their names.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
