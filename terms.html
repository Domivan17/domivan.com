Terms of Service for travellers

For the DomiVan app. Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who we are

You can reach us through Help and support in the app, or by email at support@domivan.com. We do not offer phone support.

Help and support and support@domivan.com are also our contact point for users under the EU Digital Services Act (Article 12 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065); a person on our team answers. Authorities, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services can write to us at legal@domivan.com (Article 11), in English or Dutch.

2. What DomiVan does

DomiVan sells seats in shared vans that pick you up at your door and drop you off at your destination, within and between the countries shown in the app. Trips with both the pickup and the destination in the Netherlands are not offered; trips to or from the Netherlands are.

When you book, your contract for the trip is with DomiVan: we set the price, take your payment and are responsible to you for the trip. We have the trip carried out by an independent transport company that we have checked ("your transporter"). Your transporter provides the van, the driver, the licences and permits for passenger transport, and the insurance the law requires. DomiVan does not own vans or employ drivers.

If your van has more than 9 seats including the driver's, your trip is an occasional coach service and the parts of Regulation (EU) No 181/2011 on bus and coach passengers' rights that apply to such services also apply.

3. Your account

  • You can get a price without an account. To book you sign in with your email address and a one-time code, and give your name and phone number.
  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and book. You may book for other passengers, including children travelling with an adult, and you are responsible for them following these terms.
  • Keep access to your email account safe. Anyone who can read your sign-in codes can use your DomiVan account.
  • The details you give must be correct. Your transporter uses your name and phone number to find you.

4. Booking a trip

  • You can book a Booking trip (Book ahead in the app: pickup at least 2 hours ahead, on the date and at the time you choose) or a Today trip (pickup later the same day, in a 3-hour window that starts at least 3 hours ahead). Everything these terms say about Booking trips also applies to Today trips.
  • You enter the pickup address, destination address, date and time, number of passengers and extra bags. Each passenger may bring one large bag of up to 20 kg and one small hand bag; they are included in the price. Extra bags cost extra and must be booked in advance.
  • The price shown before you pay is the full price, including VAT where it applies. There are no hidden fees.
  • Before you pay, you tick a box to confirm that you have read these terms and the privacy policy and agree with the price and these terms. We keep the time you did so with your booking.
  • Your booking is confirmed once your payment goes through. Your trip is then offered to transporters.
  • A Booking trip has a pickup window: your transporter picks you up within 2 hours from the time you choose, or within the 3-hour window you choose for a Today trip. The app shows the planned time once a transporter accepts.
  • Vans are shared, door to door. Your trip may be put together with other DomiVan travellers' trips in one van, and the driver may make other pickups and drop-offs on the way. When we do this, no traveller rides more than 15% further or 20% longer than going straight to their destination, and the price you paid stays the same.
  • DomiVan cannot guarantee that a transporter will accept every trip. If nobody accepts in time, we cancel the trip and refund you in full (see section 6).

5. Payment

  • You pay DomiVan in advance through Stripe, our payment provider, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or another method shown in the app. We never see or store your full card number.
  • DomiVan pays your transporter for carrying out the trip.
  • Prices are in euros. Your bank may charge a currency conversion fee.
  • After the trip you get DomiVan's invoice by email and on the trip in the app. You can add your company name, VAT number and address under Personal details so they appear on the invoice.
  • Invite vouchers: when a friend signs up with your invite code and their first paid trip is done, you get a voucher for 10% off a later trip, at most €20. It is used on your next booking by itself, one voucher per trip. A voucher is valid for 12 months, belongs to your account only, cannot be passed on or exchanged for money, and comes back if that trip's payment is refunded in full before the voucher expires. DomiVan may end the invite programme at any time and may cancel vouchers obtained by abuse, such as inviting yourself. If we end the invite programme, vouchers you have already earned stay valid until they expire. If you cancel a trip and only the cancellation charge is kept, its voucher comes back too.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before a transporter accepts: you can cancel in the app at any time. You get everything back except a cancellation charge of €3.59, which covers the costs a cancellation causes us. Until your payment has gone through, cancelling costs nothing.
  • Nobody accepts: if you book a Booking trip at least 25 hours before pickup, we offer it to transporters until 24 hours before pickup. If nobody has accepted it by then, we cancel it, refund your full payment and tell you in the app and by email. If you book a Booking trip closer to pickup and no transporter has accepted it 1 hour after you paid, we tell you in the app and by email. You then choose: we keep looking until the time shown in the app, never later than 1 hour before pickup, or you get a full refund right away; choosing the refund is not a cancellation by you. Without an answer we keep looking. A Booking trip that nobody accepts by then is cancelled automatically and refunded in full.
  • After a transporter accepts: you can cancel in the app until pickup. Before you confirm, the app shows what you get back, why, and when it arrives. It depends on how long before the agreed pickup time you cancel: 48 hours or more: everything back except the cancellation charge of €3.59; from 48 to 24 hours: 75% back; less than 24 hours: 50% back; from the agreed pickup time: no refund, as when you do not show up. Part of the amount we keep goes to the transporter, who had reserved seats for you. The refund goes back to the card or account you paid with.
  • Trips booked earlier: a trip booked under an earlier version of these terms, including a Live trip, keeps the cancellation rules of that version.
  • Transporter gives the trip back: we offer it to other transporters right away and tell you. While it waits for a new transporter you can cancel for a full refund, and if nobody accepts in time it is refunded in full, as above.
  • Transporter does not show up: contact us. We try to find another transporter, and if we cannot, you get a full refund.
  • When DomiVan or your transporter cancels a trip after it was accepted, you always get a full refund.
  • You do not show up: if you are not at the pickup address within 15 minutes after the agreed pickup time and your transporter cannot reach you by phone or chat, the transporter may leave and the price is not refunded.
  • Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Stripe usually takes 5 to 10 working days.
  • No right of withdrawal. Under EU consumer law (Article 3(3)(k) of Directive 2011/83/EU), the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to passenger transport. Your rights to cancel are the ones in this section.

7. Before and during the trip

  • About 24 hours before pickup (right away when a transporter accepts later than that), you and your transporter see each other's name and phone number, and can chat in the app.
  • When your transporter sets off towards you, you can follow their location in the app until you are picked up.
  • The pickup time is approximate. Long-distance trips depend on traffic, borders and other passengers, so the transporter may be earlier or later. Please keep your phone on.
  • You must carry a valid passport or ID card and any visa or documents needed for the countries you travel through. The transporter may refuse a passenger who does not have them.
  • Follow the driver's instructions, wear a seat belt, and do not smoke, drink alcohol or behave aggressively in the vehicle. You may not bring illegal, dangerous or prohibited items.
  • The transporter may refuse or stop a trip for a passenger who is a danger to others. In that case no refund is due.

8. Messages, reviews and reports

Use the chat only for your trip. Do not harass, threaten or insult anyone, and do not use DomiVan to arrange trips outside the app with transporters you met through it. Do not post anything illegal in chat messages, reviews or your profile photo. We may suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

Ratings and reviews come only from travellers whose trip was completed. You can report a chat message, review or profile photo you think is illegal or breaks these terms with Report in the app, or through Help and support. We confirm your report, a person on our team looks at it, and we tell you what we decided. If we remove something of yours or restrict your account, we tell you why and how you can object. We tell you in the app and by email. People without an account can report through the form on domivan.com.

9. Problems and complaints

When your transporter arrives, tell them the 4-digit pickup code shown in the app once you are in the van, not before. After the trip you can tap Report a problem in the app for 24 hours; we then hold the transporter's payment while we look into it.

If something goes wrong, tell us through Help and support as soon as possible, preferably within 30 days after the trip. We still look at later complaints, but it may then be harder to check what happened. We look into it and answer in the app.

Lost items, meaning things you left behind in the van after the trip, are handled between you and the transporter; we help you contact them. Luggage lost or damaged during the trip falls under section 10.

DomiVan has not joined an out-of-court dispute body. If we cannot solve your complaint together, the European Consumer Centre in your country can help you for free with a complaint about a business in another EU country, and you can go to the competent court (see section 13).

10. Liability

  • DomiVan is responsible to you for the trip you booked, also for the transporter we use to carry it out.
  • If you are injured in an accident during the trip, or your luggage is lost or damaged during the trip, you have the right to compensation under the law that applies, within the limits that law sets. The van's compulsory motor insurance covers injury to passengers, and you can also claim directly from that insurer.
  • We are not liable for money, jewellery or other valuables in your luggage that you did not hand to the driver for safekeeping.
  • For other damage, our liability for one booking is limited to the price of that booking, except where the damage was caused by our intent or gross negligence, or where the law does not allow a limit.
  • DomiVan does not provide travel insurance.

How we use your personal data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

12. Changes and ending your account

We may change these terms. We tell you in the app at least 30 days before an important change takes effect; bookings already made keep the terms that applied when you paid. You can delete your account at any time, see Delete your account. We may close your account if you seriously or repeatedly break these terms.

13. Law and courts

These terms are governed by Dutch law. If you are a consumer living in another EU country, you keep the protection of the mandatory consumer law of your country, and you may bring a claim in the courts where you live. Otherwise, disputes go to the competent court in the district of Limburg (Rechtbank Limburg).
