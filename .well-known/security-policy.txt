DomiVan: reporting a security problem

DomiVan (StepsNest B.V., the Netherlands) runs domivan.com and the DomiVan, DomiVan Driver and
DomiVan Admin apps. If you think you found a security problem in any of them, tell us.

How to report
- Email security@domivan.com: what you found, the steps to see it, and the page, app and version.
- Write in English or in any language of domivan.com.
- We aim to answer within 5 working days and tell you when it is fixed.

While you look
- Use only accounts of your own. Do not open, change or delete other people's data; if you reach
  any by accident, stop and tell us, and keep none of it.
- No denial of service, spam, social engineering, or scans that load our systems.
- Make no payments you do not mean to make, and do not test against our payment providers.
- Give us reasonable time to fix a problem before you publish anything about it.

We will not take legal action against good-faith reports that follow these rules.

DomiVan has no paid bug bounty.
