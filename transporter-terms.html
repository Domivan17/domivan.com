Terms of Service for transporters

For the DomiVan Driver app. Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

DomiVan is run by StepsNest B.V., registered in the Netherlands under Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number 42105882, registered office Sprangstraat 10, 6004 XD Weert, VAT number NL869759164B01 ("DomiVan", "we", "us"). You reach us through Help and support in the app or at support@domivan.com.

Help and support and support@domivan.com are also our contact point for users under the EU Digital Services Act (Article 12 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065); a person on our team answers. Authorities, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services can write to us at legal@domivan.com (Article 11), in English or Dutch.

These terms apply between DomiVan and the transport company or self-employed driver that signs up in the DomiVan Driver app ("you", "transporter"). They are business-to-business terms.

2. What DomiVan does for you

DomiVan finds travellers who want door-to-door van trips, sets the fare, takes their payment, and offers the trip to transporters in the app. DomiVan sells each trip to the traveller in its own name and buys the transport from you: when you accept a trip, you carry it out for DomiVan. You decide which trips you accept. You are an independent business: you are not employed by DomiVan, you are not our agent, and you choose when and how often you work.

3. Joining

  • Joining is free. There is no subscription or sign-up fee. If we add a paid plan later, it will be optional and offered separately.
  • You sign up with your email, name, phone, company name, van plate and number of passenger seats. We check your details before you can accept trips, and we may ask for documents, for example your company registration, your licence for international passenger transport (EU Community licence under Regulation (EC) No 1073/2009, or the licence that applies to you), driving licences, vehicle registration and insurance certificates. For each van's insurance we also record the insurer, the policy number, the expiry date, the cover for injury, the countries covered and whether it covers carrying paying passengers, and we check them when we approve the van.
  • To be paid you set up a payout account with Stripe, our payment provider, in Stripe's form in the DomiVan Driver app (or on Stripe's own page if the form does not open). Stripe may ask for identity and bank details and has its own terms (the Stripe Connected Account Agreement), which you accept when you set up payouts. The company Stripe verifies on your payout account must be the company you signed up with; if it is another one, your trips and payouts wait until we have checked it.
  • We may decline your application, for example if documents are missing, out of date or false, if your vans or insurance do not meet section 4, or after serious complaints. If we do, we tell you why in the app, and you can reply through support. Removing a transporter later is covered by section 10.
  • Tax details (DAC7): EU law (Council Directive 2011/16/EU, as amended by Council Directive (EU) 2021/514) requires us to collect and check your tax details and report them once a year to the Dutch Tax Administration (Belastingdienst), with the payouts, the number of trips and the amounts we took off your payouts in each quarter. You give them in the DomiVan Driver app, under Tax details: your tax identification number (TIN) and the country that issued it, your business registration number, your main address and, if you are self-employed, your date of birth. If they are still missing after our first request, two reminders and 60 days, we hold your payouts until you give them (section 8).

4. What you promise

  • You hold, and keep valid, every licence, permit, registration and authorisation needed to carry passengers for payment on the routes you accept, in every country you drive through, including the United Kingdom and Switzerland.
  • You have, at your own cost, the insurance required by law for carrying passengers, including motor third-party liability insurance that covers carrying paying passengers in every country you drive through.
  • For Large and Luxury vans, and for every bundle run (section 5a), you also have, at your own cost, carrier-liability or luggage cover for your passengers and their luggage, not only passenger accident cover. The cover names DomiVan as insured or waives recourse against DomiVan, where your insurer allows. Passenger accident insurance is optional. DomiVan does not insure you, your vehicle, your drivers or your passengers.
  • For a van with 9 or more passenger seats, you hold a valid EU Community licence (Regulation (EC) No 1073/2009), approved in the app, for single trips as well as for bundle runs.
  • Your vans are roadworthy, registered, inspected and have a seat and seat belt for every passenger. You never carry more passengers than seats.
  • Your drivers hold the right licence, follow driving and rest-time rules (including tachograph rules where they apply), and do not drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • You treat passengers with respect, do not discriminate, and follow the law, including border, customs and tax rules.
  • You pay your own taxes and social contributions and declare your income from DomiVan.

5. Offers and accepting trips

  • How trips reach you: after a traveller pays, every trip is shown in Offers at the same time to all verified transporters with payouts set up, enough seats, the documents the van needs for that trip (section 4) and trip preferences (start point, distance, countries) that match it, and push alerts follow the same rule. No one pays for a better place. Bundle runs go first to DomiVan's VIT partners for a limited time (section 5a).
  • The amount you are offered starts below the top price and rises over time (over 15 minutes for Booking trips, over 2 minutes for Live trips) up to the trip's top price. Trips that nobody has taken stay available at the top price.
  • The first transporter to tap Accept gets the trip at the amount shown at that moment. That amount is your payout for the trip; it does not change afterwards.
  • By accepting, you commit to carrying out the trip yourself, with your own vehicle and driver, at the agreed time. You may not pass the trip to another company without our written consent.
  • About 24 hours before pickup (right away for Live trips) you see the traveller's name and phone number and can chat with them in the app. Use these details only for that trip.
  • Booking trips have a pickup window: pick the traveller up within 2 hours from the time they chose. When you take a bundle run, its page shows the planned pickup time for each traveller.

5a. Bundle runs

  • DomiVan may put several Booking trips into one bundle run for one van: all pickups first, then the drop-offs, in the order shown. A run is offered first to DomiVan's VIT partners, then to every transporter it suits, for a limited time each. Trips no one takes as a run go to Offers one by one.
  • You see the run's stops (towns and planned times), the number of travellers and one total amount for the whole run. The first transporter to tap Accept the run gets every trip in it; you cannot take only part of a run. Each trip then has its own page, pickup code and payout, and the run's total is split between its trips.
  • Your vehicle must have enough seats for everyone in the run and room for their bags. Every run needs the carrier-liability or luggage cover in section 4, and a vehicle with 9 or more passenger seats needs a valid EU Community licence approved in the app. Long runs need two drivers so that every driver keeps to the driving and rest times that apply.
  • You are responsible for the licences and permits each country on the route asks of you and your vehicle.
  • A run can only be given back as a whole, before anyone in it is picked up. The rules in section 7 apply to each of its trips, including the compensation per trip from 48 hours before pickup.

6. During the trip

  • When you set off to the traveller, tap Towards client. From then until the traveller is picked up, the app shares your location with that traveller, so the app needs location access while it is open.
  • When the traveller gets in, ask for the 4-digit pickup code shown in their app and enter it to confirm the pickup. Without the code the trip cannot start. Tap Complete trip when you have dropped them off at the destination.
  • If the traveller is not at the pickup address within 15 minutes after the agreed pickup time and does not answer your call and chat message, tell us through support before you leave. Once we have confirmed the no-show (from your call and chat attempts and what you tell support), you receive your full payout for the trip, at the usual time.
  • You may refuse a passenger who is a danger to others, has no valid travel documents, or carries illegal or dangerous items. Tell us through support right away.

7. Giving a trip back

  • If you cannot do a trip you accepted, give it back in the app as soon as you know: tap Give this trip back at the bottom of the trip page, at any time before the traveller is picked up. The trip goes back to Offers for other transporters and we tell the traveller. You cannot take that trip again.
  • Giving a trip back is free until 48 hours before the pickup time. From 48 hours before pickup, and always for a Live trip, you owe DomiVan fixed compensation of €25 for the costs your late give-back causes (finding another transporter or cancelling the traveller's trip). Before you confirm, the app shows whether it applies and until when giving the trip back is free.
  • The compensation is due 24 hours after you give the trip back. It is not payment for a service, so no VAT is charged on it. We send you a statement and take it off your next payouts until it is paid in full. We may waive it, for example after an emergency; if it was already on a statement, you get a correction.
  • If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 112 first. Late give-backs and no-shows hurt travellers, so only accept trips you can do.
  • If you give back trips late (within 48 hours of pickup, or a Live trip) or do not show up, three times within 90 days, we may suspend your account for 30 days: while it is suspended you get no new trips and your payouts are held (sections 8 and 10). After five times within 12 months we may end your account with 30 days' notice. Each time we tell you the reason in the app and by email, and you can reply through support.

8. Payment

  • The traveller pays DomiVan. DomiVan sends your payout for a trip to your Stripe account 24 hours after you tap Complete trip, or 72 hours after it while you or the traveller have had fewer than 3 trips paid out, unless the traveller reports a problem before then; then the payout waits until we have looked into it. Stripe then pays it to your bank account on its normal payout schedule.
  • If the traveller cancels after you accepted, you receive the same share of the amount we keep under the traveller terms (50% of the fare when they cancel between 48 and 12 hours before pickup, the full fare within 12 hours) as your payout is of the full fare. Nothing is due if they cancel 48 hours or more before pickup. A Live trip is free for the traveller to cancel within 5 minutes after you accept it, before you tap Towards client, and then nothing is due either. When a traveller cancels a Live trip later, we check the cancellation before your share is paid, and we may refund the traveller instead. DomiVan sends your share to your Stripe account 24 hours after the cancellation (72 hours under the same rule as above), unless we pause it to look into a problem.
  • Your payout is the amount you accepted. It is the whole price for your transport and includes any VAT you owe on it, except Dutch VAT that is reverse-charged to DomiVan (see self-billing below). You pay DomiVan nothing, except the give-back compensation (section 7) and confirmed claims (below), which come off your payouts; each comes with a statement of its own, and the self-billing invoice for a payout says how much of it was set off against them.
  • If a trip was not carried out, was marked complete by mistake, or a traveller's complaint is upheld (for example the trip was not done or the passenger was left behind), we may withhold or reclaim the payout for that trip, after hearing your side.
  • Claims: if a traveller, an authority or anyone else claims from DomiVan for something caused by your carriage, drivers or vehicles (section 11), we send you a notice with the evidence, and you have 14 days to reply. A claim we confirm after hearing you is compensation you owe DomiVan, outside the scope of VAT. We send you a statement for it and take it off your later payouts and VIT cash-outs until it is paid in full; it never lowers the payout on your self-billing invoice. While a serious claim against you is open, we may hold your payouts.
  • If the traveller's bank disputes the card payment for a trip, or reports it as fraud, after your payout for it went out, we may take that payout back from your Stripe account. If you are a VIT partner, what you earned from that trip through an invite is taken back too, from your next cash-out if you already cashed it out.
  • We hold all your payouts and VIT cash-outs while your account is suspended, while the company on your Stripe payout account is not the company you signed up with, while we look into suspected fraud, while your tax details are missing (section 3) or while a serious claim against you is open, until it is resolved.
  • You agree to self-billing. For each payout DomiVan issues the invoice from you to DomiVan, in your name and on your behalf (SB- numbers, in a series of their own). You get it by email and in the app, and you do not send us invoices yourself. You accept these invoices as your own and remain responsible for the VAT on them. Each invoice shows the kilometres and the amount for each country the trip went through. Dutch VAT on the part driven in the Netherlands is reverse-charged to DomiVan when your company is not established in the Netherlands; when it is, the invoice charges 9% Dutch VAT on that part. VAT on the parts driven in other countries, if due, is yours to account for in those countries. Tell us straight away if your company or VAT details change, and tell us within 14 days if an invoice is wrong.
  • Invite rewards: once your account is verified, you get a personal invite code and link. For 1 year from the sign-up of each driver or traveller who joins with them, you earn 5% of DomiVan's net earnings on every trip that driver drives and 5% on every trip that traveller takes, except trips you drive yourself. DomiVan's net earnings on a trip are what the traveller paid, minus the transporter's payout and the payment provider's fee for that payment. Rewards are added to your next payout, as a transfer of their own, and shown on that payout's self-billing invoice. A refunded trip earns nothing, and a reward already paid for a trip whose payment is later refunded or disputed is taken off your next rewards. VIT partners earn 10% on their invited drivers' trips and 5% on their invited travellers' trips under their VIT partner agreement instead. We may change the rates for future trips, end the invite programme, or withhold rewards obtained by abuse, such as inviting yourself. An invite reward is payment for a service you provide to DomiVan: bringing it new drivers and travellers. Only businesses earn rewards. Each reward is a line of its own on the self-billing invoice of the payout it comes with. If your company is established in the Netherlands, the reward includes 21% Dutch VAT, which that invoice shows and you pay to the Belastingdienst; for every other company the VAT is reverse-charged, so DomiVan accounts for it in the Netherlands and the invoice says "Reverse charge".
  • Amounts are in euros.

9. Travellers, conduct and fair use

  • Do not ask travellers to pay you extra, in cash or otherwise, for a trip booked through DomiVan.
  • Do not use DomiVan to take travellers off the platform to book future trips directly with you. We ask this because DomiVan finds the traveller, checks the trip and pays for it; trips arranged outside the app would carry none of these protections.
  • Keep traveller data confidential and use it only for the trip. Delete phone numbers and messages you no longer need. For this data you are a separate controller under the GDPR.
  • Do not harass, threaten or insult travellers or DomiVan staff, and do not post anything illegal in chat messages or your profile photo.
  • Ratings and reviews of you come only from travellers whose trip with you was completed. You can report a chat message, review or profile photo you think is illegal or breaks these terms with Report in the app, or through Help and support. We confirm your report, a person on our team looks at it, and we tell you what we decided. If we remove something of yours or restrict your account, we tell you why and how you can object, in the app and by email.

10. Suspension and ending

You can stop using DomiVan and delete your account at any time, after finishing the trips you accepted. We may suspend or end your access immediately if you break these terms, if your documents are no longer valid, after serious complaints about safety or conduct, or for late give-backs and no-shows as section 7 says. Otherwise we give you at least 30 days' notice. Each time we tell you the reason in the app and by email, with the facts and the part of these terms it is based on, and how you can object (section 13). Payouts for trips you completed are still paid, unless section 8 applies.

11. Liability

  • Towards travellers, DomiVan is the carrier and answers for the trip under the law (traveller terms, section 10). Between you and DomiVan, you are responsible for the transport you carry out and for everything that happens during it, including damage, injury, delay and lost luggage, and for fines and costs caused by you or your drivers.
  • You indemnify DomiVan against all claims, damages, fines and costs from travellers, authorities or anyone else, to the extent caused by your carriage, drivers or vehicles, including what DomiVan pays a traveller for injury, luggage or delay. Towards DomiVan you waive the statutory limits of liability that would otherwise apply to you. Confirmed claims are taken off your payouts as section 8 says.
  • DomiVan's liability to you is limited to the payouts due for the trips concerned, and in any case to €5,000 per calendar year, except for intent or gross negligence. DomiVan is not liable for lost profit or for trips that are not offered or not accepted.

12. Your data

How we use your personal data, including your location while you drive towards a traveller, is explained in our Privacy Policy.

13. Changes, law and courts

We may change these terms with at least 15 days' notice in the app; if you do not agree, you may end your account before the change takes effect. These terms are governed by Dutch law, and disputes go to the competent court in the district of Limburg (Rechtbank Limburg).

You can complain to us about these terms, how we apply them, or a decision about your account, through Help and support. We confirm receipt, look into it and answer within 14 days; if we need longer, we tell you why. DomiVan is a small enterprise and has not named mediators.
