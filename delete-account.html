Delete your account

For the Domivan and Domivan Driver apps, run by StepsNest B.V.

How to ask

  1. Open the Domivan or Domivan Driver app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Help and support.
  3. Write "Please delete my account".

If you can no longer sign in, email [privacy@domivan.com] from the email address of your account.

We delete your account within 30 days and confirm in the app or by email. If you have a trip that is still upcoming or in progress, we first finish or cancel it under our terms.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in email, name, phone number and profile photo
  • For transporters: company name, van details and documents
  • Your chat and support messages, and any saved location
  • Your push notification token

What we keep

Records of paid trips, refunds and payouts (date, route, amounts and payment references) are kept for 7 years because Dutch tax law requires it, and then deleted. Stripe keeps its own payment and payout records under its privacy policy.
