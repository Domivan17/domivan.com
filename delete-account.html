Delete your account

For the DomiVan and DomiVan Driver apps, run by StepsNest B.V.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the DomiVan or DomiVan Driver app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Deleting waits while a paid trip of yours is still running (finish or cancel it first, see our terms) and while a payout, an invoice or a refund for you is still to come. The app tells you which, and you can delete your account once it is done.

If you can't sign in

Email privacy@domivan.com from the email address of your account and write "Please delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in email and login (a login with Apple is also revoked)
  • Your name, phone number, profile photo and push notification token
  • Your billing details for invoices: name, VAT number and address
  • For transporters: your company details (name, country, registration and VAT numbers, address and our company check), your van plate, your home base, the position the Driver app sent while you were Online, and the link to your payout account
  • For transporters: the photos of your documents (also the optional Community licence and German permit)
  • Your support messages
  • The chat translations of your trips, also of trips you gave back as a transporter (from while you had them)
  • Your entries in our sign-in security log (your email and IP address with each sign-in)
  • Applications you sent through Join Transporters on our website with the same email address as your account: your name, phone number and email, the company name, registration and VAT numbers, and our staff's notes on them
  • Notes and reasons our staff wrote about you or your trips: on your documents and applications, why your VIT deal ended, the name on the VIT invite you used, why a give-back fee of yours was waived and why a failed refund to you was closed, also in our internal staff log (see below)
  • Trips you booked but never paid for: they are cancelled, and their pickup and drop-off addresses, map coordinates and notes are deleted

What we keep

  • Records of paid trips, refunds and payouts (date, pickup and drop-off addresses, trip notes, amounts and payment references), without your name, for 7 years, because Dutch tax law requires it, and then deleted. The ratings and review comments on those trips, the transporter's company name each review is counted under, and the last position shared during a trip stay with that trip.
  • Invoices and credit notes already issued, also self-billing invoices for transporters: each keeps its own copy of the names, email addresses, addresses and company and VAT numbers on it, for 7 years, because Dutch tax law requires it.
  • For trips you booked but never paid for: the towns, date, passengers and price, without the addresses and without your name.
  • For transporters: which documents our staff approved or rejected and when, with each document's expiry date and the number of van seats it was approved for, without the photos, the van plate or staff notes.
  • For Join Transporters applications from our website: when each one came and what we decided, with the answers that do not name you (country, language, business type, licence, passenger insurance, technical inspection, fleet size, van classes, destinations and the invite code it came with), without the details listed above.
  • Invite codes: who invited whom, and the ID of the phone a code was used on, kept with the records of what the invite earned (it stops the same phone earning twice).
  • Our internal staff log: what our staff decided about your account, documents, applications and trips, and when (for example that a document was approved or a refund was made, with its amount), without personal details: no names, contact or company details, plates, messages, notes or typed reasons.
  • Chat messages about your trips, which stay with the trip record until they are deleted 1 year after the trip, as our Privacy Policy says.
  • Stripe keeps its own payment and payout records under its privacy policy.
